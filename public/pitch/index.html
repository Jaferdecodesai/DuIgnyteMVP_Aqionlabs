Aqion Relay helps a non-perishable goods distributor compare recovery purchases against an owner-defined cash floor. The public MVP uses fictional SME and supplier data. It trains a numerical forecast, evaluates feasible alternatives and saves an unsent draft only after owner confirmation. No order is sent and no funds move. The two cover links take judges directly to the public source and the verified live demonstration. Sources: S01 · S08

The forecast fits weekday and trend ridge regression to fictional fulfilled-sales history, comparing the last 14 days against a seasonal-naive baseline; it chooses the lower synthetic holdout-error method and refits as applicable. Short or constant histories use a labelled trailing-average fallback. The same holdout is used for model selection, so it is not an independent final quality test. Fulfilled sales may censor true demand. Candidate quantities obey approved-supplier status, quote expiry, MOQ, pack size, cap and lead time. Enumeration is bounded, at most 8,000 combinations in the current three-supplier slice, not a proof of a global optimum. Deterministic templates explain the calculation; no live generative model decides purchases. Sources: S08

Four waves of 40 start in pilot weeks 1,2,3,4. STOP RULES / Projected cash-floor breach · unauthorized write · duplicate external effect
All percentages on this slide are prospective pilot targets, not achieved customer outcomes. Primary outcome: time from equivalent disruption brief to owner-approved plan that passes a predetermined blinded feasibility rubric. Use baseline-adjusted log-time comparison with SME assignment, wave/calendar and strata; report effect and 95% interval plus raw medians. Include non-completion under a preregistered capped-time rule with sensitivity analysis. Target 40% shorter time. Secondary feasibility target: at least 90% rubric-passing approved plans, all assigned drills in denominator. Adoption target: 70% of SMEs with access review at least one scenario per week, reporting opportunity and reasons for non-use. Exploratory stockout target is 15% reduction, reported separately with uncertainty and no causal before/after shortcut. Zero projected floor breaches, unauthorized external writes and duplicate external effects are stop rules for the later live pilot, not claims that predicted cash guarantees actual cash. Monitor source completeness and reconciliation latency. Independent baseline variance/power analysis determines whether efficacy or feasibility interpretation is justified. Sources: S09
